Controls & court rules

WASD or on-screen arrows move. Drag the court to look; arrow keys also aim. E aims toward an opponent. Hold the Throw button, then release. With mouse lock: move to aim, hold/release left mouse to throw. Space attempts a 0.4s catch; hands must be empty. Shift gives a short speed burst. P or Esc pauses.

Live throws eliminate opponents. A floor bounce makes a ball safe. A catch eliminates the thrower and returns one teammate. Stay on your half. Balls on the floor are picked up automatically. At the buzzer, the team with more active players wins. A tie replays the round. First to two round wins takes the match.
